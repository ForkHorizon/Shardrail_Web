## 1. Research & Context
- Claude Code emits permission queries over its control stream (`canUseTool` / `permission-prompt-tool`).
- Shardrail enforces fail-closed security for all approval decisions (`ApprovalDecision::Allow`, `ApprovalDecision::Deny`, `ApprovalDecision::AskUser`).
- Timeouts, client disconnections, or unconfirmed prompts must strictly default to `Deny`.

## 2. Design & Acceptance Criteria
- **Scope & Changes**:
  - Implement pending approval tracking with `tokio::sync::oneshot` channels in `ClaudeProcessManager`.
  - Wire `resolve_approval` on `ClaudeAdapter` to dispatch decisions to waiting subprocesses.
  - Fail closed on timeouts or drops.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - [x] Permission requests cleanly route through the standard `AgentAdapter::resolve_approval` interface.
  - [x] Fail-closed: missing or dropped approval sender resolves to `Deny`.
  - [x] Full parity with Codex approval lifecycle.
